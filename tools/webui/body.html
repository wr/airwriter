Set up Basilisk II
  1. In Basilisk II’s settings, open Ports and turn on Tunnel Mac OS networking over UDP and Tunnel AppleTalk only.
  2. Start the Mac and choose EtherTalk in its Network control panel.

The second keeps the Mac on the internet. Older versions of Basilisk II don’t have it, and the Mac loses the internet while the tunnel is on. Learn More…
Set up Infinite Mac

Macs in Infinite Mac share AppleTalk in a zone you name.

Anyone who opens Infinite Mac with this zone name can print here. To use a zone you already have, type its name.
