classic macs

Classic Macs print here over AppleTalk: on the LocalTalk port, from Ethernet Macs and BlueSCSI Wi-Fi Macs on this network, and from Basilisk II with “Tunnel MacOS Networking over UDP” turned on.

Anyone with this name can print here. Open Infinite Mac with it or make a new one.

On the Mac, choose AppleTalk ImageWriter in the Chooser.
